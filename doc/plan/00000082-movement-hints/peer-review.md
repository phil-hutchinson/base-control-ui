# Peer Review — Story 00000082, The board prices the move

## Summary

The branch removes the selected ship's corner brackets and teaches the
destination and target marks to state a move's price: `legalMoves` /
`legalAttacks` hand the rules layer's `ReachEntry` (cost and all) to
`Board.tsx`, `SquareMark` becomes a cost-carrying discriminated union whose
accessible names say `costs N power`, and `BoardSquare.tsx` draws that many
copies of the hull gauge's own bar, centred, in the interaction accent. The
change is well-scoped and faithful to its documents: `rules.md`,
`changelog.md`, `RULES_VERSION`, `src/game/session.ts`,
`src/board/announcements.ts` and the Quick Guide files are all untouched (S1,
S9, S10); D3's wording is implemented verbatim; D5's geometry checks out (a
three-bar stack's outermost cap reaches ~23.9 units from centre against the
ring's 29-unit inner edge) and the stack is generated from the cost with no
ceiling (S6, pinned by a four-bar test); the accessibility ledger note from D8
is present; and no new comment cites a story number or a plan step. The two
deviations recorded in Step 6's Notes — updating `README.md` at all, and
saying "power" rather than "fuel" in it — were both the right calls: the
status paragraph already enumerates what the board shows a player, and
`README.md` says "power" throughout, so a lone "fuel" would have introduced a
second word for one substance inside one paragraph.

Verification from the repository root: `npm run typecheck` **green**,
`npm run lint` **green**, `npm test` **green** (66 files, 1293 tests, up from
the plan's 1278 baseline). `npx prettier --check .` was not required by this
review but the plan's Step 6 verification depends on it; the two pre-existing
warnings remain and are not this story's. Findings below are one Major and
six Minor; none of them is a rules discrepancy.
