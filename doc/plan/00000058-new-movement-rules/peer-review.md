# Peer Review — Story 00000058, Power becomes a fuel tank, and movement is priced

## Summary

The branch turns power into a spent reserve: `rules.md` goes to 0.24 in its own
commit with a single changelog entry, `PowerLevel` widens to 0–6, `REACH_OPTIONS`
is re-transcribed as a four-row cost table with the new L (its eight offsets and
both corners written as literal data), only enemy ships block a path, moves and
attacks deduct the shape's cost through checked `spendPower`/`gainPower` helpers,
the end-of-turn step 1 becomes the planet gain alone at one or two a turn, and
the gauge is redrawn as six lines in two rows of three with unlit slots drawing
nothing. All eleven plan steps are marked committed, with their deviations
recorded in Notes; the rules layer, its tests and the announcement wording all
follow the plan closely.

`npm run typecheck` passes. `npm run lint` passes. `npm test` passes (966 tests,
54 files). The findings below are therefore all from reading the diff: one
sentence in `rules.md` still states the retired charged-node drain, `README.md`
misstates two rules to players, and there is a cluster of comment-convention
breaches (plan-step and decision citations, and narration of a rejected
approach) that `CONTRIBUTING.md` puts in `doc/plan/` rather than in source.
