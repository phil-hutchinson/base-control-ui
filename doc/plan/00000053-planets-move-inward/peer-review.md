# Peer Review — 00000053 Planets move inward

## Summary

The branch replaces the fourteen edge bays with twelve interior planets,
renames "bay" to "planet" throughout the rules, the app and the code, drops
fleets to five or six a side, trims the drawing catalogue to twelve dealt at
random from `GameState.openingSeed`, teaches the node draw to keep off planets
and their neighbours, and takes `NODE_COUNT` from fifteen to twelve. The
settled geometry (B3, D6, G4, J2, K6, N4 plus half-turn rotations) was verified
independently: no two planets are adjacent, none sits on a starting square, the
set is closed under a half-turn, six of the twelve lie inside C3–M13, the legal
node pool is exactly 51 squares and its largest mutually non-adjacent subset is
18 — all matching `rules.md` 0.23, the changelog and `RULES_VERSION`.

`npm run typecheck` passed (exit 0). `npm run lint` passed (exit 0).
`npm test` passed (exit 0) — 54 files, 933 tests. No finding is filed against
those three.
